# Data and attribution

NutriFacts is free to use. The application code is licensed under the MIT License. New, original data contributions to this project are proposed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), which allows reuse, including commercial reuse, when attribution and license terms are followed.

Each data record must keep its own source citation, source URL or persistent identifier, and source license. A record keeps the original source's terms when those terms differ. The project only redistributes third-party data when its license permits redistribution and the record's attribution requirements are met. A public webpage or downloadable file is not automatically open data.

The starter food record comes from USDA FoodData Central, whose data are public domain under CC0. The source, food identifier, measurement method, sample count, and converted display units are kept with the record. The displayed per-gram values are conversions of the source's per-100-gram values; they do not imply that every food sample has the same composition.

Before submitting, contributors must have the right to share the data they add under the proposed license and must cite the evidence. Do not copy a source's figures, tables, images, or dataset into NutriFacts unless its terms allow that reuse. Linking to a source is welcome even when its data cannot be redistributed.

FooDB is useful as an external discovery source, but its current terms restrict commercial reuse. NutriFacts must not import or redistribute FooDB records unless the project obtains permission or the terms change. See [FooDB's terms](https://foodb.ca/about).

Optional donations may support project maintenance. They never unlock features or data and do not change the free-use terms.
